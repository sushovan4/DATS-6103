Modeling Methodology


9 minutes

Machine learning modeling typically follows these steps:

1. Splitting the Data

Before building models, it’s essential to evaluate their performance on unseen data. A common approach is to split the dataset into two parts:

  • Training set: used to train the model.
  • Test set: used to evaluate the model’s generalization performance.

A typical split is 70–80% for training and 20–30% for testing.

Exercises

Split it yourself

train_test_split is three lines of numpy, and writing them once is the way to stop treating it as magic. Shuffle the row indices with the generator provided, take the first 80\% for training and the rest for testing, and store the two index arrays as train_idx and test_idx.

cut = int(0.8 * n)
train_idx = order[:cut]
test_idx = order[cut:]
Five folds, by hand

Cross-validation reuses every row as test data exactly once. Split the shuffled order into 5 folds with np.array_split and store the list as folds.

folds = np.array_split(order, 5)
Accuracy is not enough

Out of 1000 patients, 20 have the disease. A model that predicts “healthy” for everyone is given below. Compute its accuracy, and its recall on the sick patients—the fraction of genuinely sick people it identifies.

accuracy = (y_pred == y_true).mean()
recall = (y_pred[y_true == 1] == 1).mean()
